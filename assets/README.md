# RAMBA assets

concepts/ contains original AI-generated fictional resort scenes in WebP: hero, villa, exterior, interior, pool, garden, dining, evening, lifestyle and texture. Each has 640, 960 and 1600 pixel landscape variants. Exterior, interior and texture also have 640 × 800 portrait crops.

The high-resolution generated PNG originals (1672 × 941) are in design/originals/. They are design source assets, excluded from dist/. See design/IMAGERY.md for prompts and provenance. The scenes do not document actual RAMBA architecture, surroundings or facilities.

fonts/ holds self-hosted DM Sans and Playfair Display with their upstream SIL Open Font Licences. Font files came from Google Fonts; licence texts came from the official google/fonts repository.

Replace concept scenes through src/imagery.mjs only after actual imagery is approved. Retain disclosures while generated scenes remain. The official transparent/vector RAMBA logo is still required; the visible brand is plain text pending that asset.
