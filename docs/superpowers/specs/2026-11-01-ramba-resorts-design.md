# RAMBA website design — updated 2 November 2026

The latest user brief supersedes the earlier homepage architecture. Home now has seven main sections and a shared footer. Story 4, Experience 5, Retreat 4 and Enquire 3 remain intact pending homepage approval.

Original AI imagery establishes an explicitly conceptual atmosphere. The opening arch expands into a forest pathway scene. Editorial typography, asymmetrical image studies, a sticky evening scene and an accessible four-moment selector provide varied pacing. CSS motion respects system and manual preferences. No animation library or runtime framework is required.

Actual property facts remain limited to completed villa exteriors and interiors/pool under construction. No bookings, prices, opening dates, room specifications, ownership offers or operational facilities are invented. The enquiry flow remains local and the email stays stay@ramba.example.
