## Shared creative direction

Fictional resort concepts, never presented as an existing property. Restrained contemporary architecture: low sandstone volumes, warm lime plaster, timber screens, earthen terracotta, mature forest and meadow grasses. Warm sand and deep forest-green grading, muted brass and charcoal shadows. Professional editorial architectural photography, natural realistic light, plausible structure, fine natural textures, no CGI gloss, excessive saturation, lettering, watermark or logo. Landscape compositions with some negative space for typography.
