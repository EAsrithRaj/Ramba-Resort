02 / Before you ask

A few
clear things.

Is RAMBA open?

The villa exteriors are complete. Interiors and the pool are under construction. An opening date has not been confirmed.

Can I make a reservation?

There is no booking or live availability service here. Preparing a draft does not reserve a room or submit an enquiry.

Is that really the photography?

No. All resort imagery is AI-generated and conceptual. It does not show the actual RAMBA property or completed facilities. Approved photography and footage will replace it when available.

Where does my information go?

It stays in this page until you close or reload it. Saving a draft creates a text file on your device. Opening your mail app transfers the draft into your mail client; sending remains your choice.
